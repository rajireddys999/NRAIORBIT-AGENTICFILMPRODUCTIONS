Privacy policy

{{ settings.title }} · {{ origin }}

This page is the private production console of a single film studio. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the studio's own accounts.

What it stores

  • Sign-in. When you sign in with Google, the page receives your Google account's e-mail address to check it against the studio's allow-list, and keeps a signed session cookie in your browser for up to seven days. Nothing else from your Google account is read or kept.
  • YouTube. When the studio connects its own channel, Google issues an upload token for that channel. The token is stored in Google Cloud Secret Manager inside the studio's own project and is used only to upload the studio's films and their captions to that channel. The channel's name and the time of connection are recorded.
  • Film data. Stories, scripts, images, video, audio and reports made for the studio's films live in the studio's private Google Cloud storage bucket. They belong to the studio.

What it does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no sale or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No access to anyone else's Google or YouTube account.

Removal

The studio can revoke the page's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the stored token and records from its Google Cloud project.
