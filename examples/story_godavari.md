# Story prompt: గోదావరి సింహం (Godavari Simham)

On the banks of the Godavari, a ruthless boss named Bhairava taxes the fishermen and burns
the boats of anyone who refuses. Arjun, a quiet young man who returned to the village after
years away, stands up to him. Meera, a school teacher who loves Arjun, fears for his life.

At the interval, Bhairava discovers that Arjun is the son of Raghavaiah, the honest
fishermen's leader he murdered years ago. In the second half, a flashback reveals the night
Raghavaiah died. Bhairava burns every boat in the village, and Arjun's mother falls ill
from the shock. The village, which had always stayed silent, finally rallies behind Arjun.
The climax is a torch-lit confrontation on the riverbank during the Godavari Pushkaralu
festival.

Tone: mass action with strong mother sentiment and a light romance track. Comedy from
Arjun's friend Suri, a talkative auto driver.
